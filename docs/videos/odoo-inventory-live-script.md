# Video script: Odoo inventory, recorded live

Animated intro from the Odoo inventory course, then a continuous live screen recording in an Odoo 20 database (fictional Northwind data), then a recap. Video /videos/odoo-inventory-live.mp4 in the portal. Narration: Microsoft neural voice Neerja (Indian English).

| # | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title card: **Odoo Inventory: Warehouses, Routes and Stock**. A short introduction, then the full process recorded live in Odoo 20. | Welcome to Odoo Inventory. First a short introduction, and then we will receive a partial delivery live in Odoo, with a backorder for the rest. |
| 2 | **Where every unit is, and what it is worth**. Cards: Locations · Transfers · Valuation | The Odoo Inventory app records where every unit is, how it got there, and what it is worth. It is a double entry stock system: every movement is a stock move from one location to another. Purchase creates the receipts, Sales creates the deliveries, and Accounting values the stock. |
| 3 | **What changes for a company like Northwind**. Cards: Real-time stock · Fewer stock-outs · Partial deliveries · Known stock value | For a company like Northwind Office Supplies this means real time stock per location, reordering rules against stock outs of toner, automatic backorders when a vendor delivers only part of an order, and a known stock value at month end. |
| 4 | **From purchase order to stock report**. Animated flow: Purchase Order ▸ Receipt ▸ Backorder ▸ On hand ▸ Delivery ▸ Stock report | Here is the flow: the purchase order creates a receipt, the receipt brings stock on hand, and any missing quantity stays open as a backorder until it arrives. Our scenario: Paper Mills delivers three hundred of the five hundred reams ordered. Let us do it live in Odoo. |
| 5 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Open the receipt** | Let us do it live. In Inventory, open Receipts and click the receipt from Paper Mills for five hundred reams. |
| 6 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Record what arrived** | Only three hundred reams were on the truck. Change the quantity on the line to three hundred. |
| 7 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Validate** | Click Validate. Odoo notices that two hundred are missing and asks what to do. |
| 8 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Create backorder** | Paper Mills will deliver the rest next week, so create a backorder. Three hundred reams enter stock now. |
| 9 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Stock report** | Finally open the stock report. On hand has gone up by three hundred, and the backorder shows as incoming. That is a receipt with a backorder, live in Odoo. |
| 10 | **What you just did in Odoo**. Animated flow: Open the receipt ▸ Record 300 received ▸ Validate ▸ Create backorder ▸ Check the stock report | To recap: you opened the receipt, recorded three hundred reams received, validated it, created a backorder for the missing two hundred, and checked the stock report. Now practise it yourself with the click-through simulators in the Odoo Inventory course. |
