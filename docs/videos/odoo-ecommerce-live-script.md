# Video script: Odoo ecommerce, recorded live

Animated intro from the Odoo ecommerce course, then a continuous live screen recording in an Odoo 20 database (fictional Northwind data), then a recap. Video /videos/odoo-ecommerce-live.mp4 in the portal. Narration: Microsoft neural voice Neerja (Indian English).

| # | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title card: **Odoo eCommerce and E-Invoicing**. A short introduction, then the full process recorded live in Odoo 20. | Welcome to Odoo eCommerce. First a short introduction, and then we will process a web shop order live in Odoo, from payment to shipping and invoice. |
| 2 | **One database behind the web shop**. Cards: Web shop · Sales order · Invoice and e-invoice | In Odoo, the web shop runs on the same database as Sales, Inventory and Accounting. The customer browses, adds items to the cart and checks out, and every web order becomes a sales order. Behind it, the retailer still reserves stock, ships, and issues a legally valid invoice, increasingly as a structured electronic invoice. |
| 3 | **What changes for an online retailer**. Cards: One system · Automatic invoices · Correct VAT · Structured e-invoices | For an online retailer this means the shop, stock and accounting live in one system, invoices are created automatically, the right V A T applies for every delivery country, and business customers receive structured e-invoices instead of P D Fs. |
| 4 | **From checkout to invoice**. Animated flow: Checkout ▸ Web Order ▸ Payment ▸ Pick ▸ Deliver ▸ Invoice | Here is the flow: checkout, web order, payment, pick, delivery and invoice. Our scenario: Lea Martens places an order in the web shop and pays by wire transfer, so the payment is confirmed before the goods are shipped. Let us do it live in Odoo. |
