# Video script: Odoo accounting, recorded live

Animated intro from the Odoo accounting course, then a continuous live screen recording in an Odoo 20 database (fictional Northwind data), then a recap. Video /videos/odoo-accounting-live.mp4 in the portal. Narration: Microsoft neural voice Neerja (Indian English).

| # | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title card: **Odoo Accounting: Invoice to Payment**. A short introduction, then the full process recorded live in Odoo 20. | Welcome to Odoo Accounting. First a short introduction, and then we will invoice a customer and register the payment live in Odoo. |
| 2 | **Where every transaction ends up**. Cards: Customer invoice · Payment · Bank reconciliation | Accounting is where every operational transaction in Odoo ends up. A sales invoice, a vendor bill, a stock receipt or a payment all become journal entries, made of debit and credit lines. Odoo Community has the Invoicing app, and Odoo Enterprise adds the full Accounting app with financial reports and the bank reconciliation widget. |
| 3 | **What changes for a company like Northwind**. Cards: No retyped invoices · Who owes what · Faster bank matching · Faster close | For a company like Northwind Office Supplies this means no more invoices typed in Word, a clear view of who owes what, faster bank matching instead of Excel, and a month end that becomes a review instead of fifteen days of data entry. |
| 4 | **From invoice to cash**. Animated flow: Sales Order ▸ Draft Invoice ▸ Posted ▸ Register Payment ▸ In Payment ▸ Reconciled | Here is the flow: a draft invoice is posted, the payment is registered, the invoice shows In Payment, and it becomes Paid when the bank statement line is reconciled. Our scenario: Contoso buys twelve toner cartridges and pays by bank transfer. Let us do it live in Odoo. |
| 5 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **New invoice** | Let us do it live. In Accounting, open Customer Invoices and click New. |
| 6 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Customer** | Select the customer Contoso. Payment terms and the receivable account come from the contact. |
| 7 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Invoice line** | Add a line, select the HP toner cartridge, and set the quantity to twelve. GST is added automatically. |
| 8 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Post** | Check the totals and click Confirm. The invoice gets its number and the journal entry is posted. |
| 9 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Register payment** | Contoso pays by bank transfer. Click Pay, check the amount and the journal, and create the payment. |
| 10 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **In Payment** | The invoice now shows In Payment. After the bank reconciliation it becomes Paid. That is invoice to cash, live in Odoo. |
| 11 | **What you just did in Odoo**. Animated flow: Customer invoice ▸ Add the line ▸ Post ▸ Register payment ▸ In Payment | To recap: you created the customer invoice, added the toner line, posted it, and registered the bank transfer. The invoice now shows In Payment until the bank statement line is reconciled. Now practise it yourself with the click-through simulators in the Odoo Accounting course. |
