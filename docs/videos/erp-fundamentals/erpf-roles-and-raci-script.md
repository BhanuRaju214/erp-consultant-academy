# Video script: Project Roles and RACI

ERP Fundamentals lesson video (/videos/erpf-roles-and-raci.mp4), shown at the top of the lesson. Built from the lesson content; Odoo colours, nexusEPM logo, narration by Microsoft neural voice Neerja (Indian English) with captions.

| # | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title card: **Project Roles and RACI**. Who does the work, who owns the decision, and how to stop projects stalling. | Welcome to lesson ten of E R P Fundamentals. In this video you will meet the people in an E R P project, on the client side, the partner side and the vendor side, and learn the R A C I matrix, the simple tool that makes clear who does the work and who owns each decision. |
| 2 | Step 6 wk: **Nobody could approve pricing** (who, Odoo menu, impact) with an animated **Pricing design** card | At Sunrise Retail Group, a chain of twelve stores, the E R P project stalled for six weeks because nobody could approve the pricing design. Sales said finance owned it. Finance said it was a sales decision. The implementation partner simply waited. Clear roles, and a R A C I matrix, would have prevented all of this. |
| 3 | **The client's roles**. Cards: Executive sponsor · Steering committee · Client PM · Process owners · Key users · End users · Internal IT | On the client side, the executive sponsor owns the business case, funds the project and resolves escalations. The steering committee meets monthly to review progress, risks and major decisions. The client project manager coordinates internal people and timelines. Process owners are department heads responsible for a process. Key users, also called super users, attend workshops, test, help with data and train colleagues; after sponsorship, they are the most important success factor. Then come the end users and internal I T. |
| 4 | **Who supports the client**. Cards: Partner PM · Consultants · Developers · Solution architect · Vendor | The implementation partner brings a project manager who plans, tracks and reports and manages scope and change requests, plus functional and techno-functional consultants for discovery, design, configuration, testing and training. Developers build customizations, integrations and migration scripts, and a solution architect leads the overall design on complex projects. Finally, the software vendor supplies licences, product support, hosting for cloud subscriptions, and bug fixes for the standard product. |
| 5 | **Four letters, four kinds of involvement**. Cards: R — Responsible · A — Accountable · C — Consulted · I — Informed | R A C I stands for four kinds of involvement. Responsible means the person who does the work, and every task needs at least one. Accountable means the person who owns the outcome and signs it off, and there must be exactly one. Consulted people give their input before or during the work, in a two-way conversation. Informed people are simply kept up to date, one way. |
| 6 | Who is accountable for what? | Here is part of a sample R A C I for an E R P project. Notice two patterns. First, the client is accountable for data cleansing and user acceptance testing. Partners can help, but only the business knows whether its data and processes are correct. Second, the sponsor is accountable for the go-live decision, based on readiness evidence prepared by the two project managers. The partner is accountable for delivery work like configuration and the customization build. |
| 7 | Common role problems | These are the common role problems. If key users are not released from daily work, workshops get cancelled and testing is poor, so agree a time allocation of around thirty to fifty percent in the project charter. With no single accountable owner, decisions stall. A disengaged sponsor leaves escalations unresolved. If the partner decides business rules, the design will not fit the business. And if I T joins late, expect surprises with network, security and integrations. |
| 8 | Step 1 wk: **Exactly one A** (who, Odoo menu, impact) with an animated **Pricing design** card | A tempting mistake is to make two people accountable, say the commercial director and the finance director, thinking more owners means more control. In fact, with two A's nobody can make the final call. Sunrise restarted with a R A C I agreed at the steering committee. The commercial director was accountable, finance was consulted, and store managers were informed. A decision log was created. The pricing design was signed off within one week, and the log later answered why questions during testing. |
| 9 | **Key takeaways**. Cards: Know the roles · Exactly one A · Client owns business calls · Release key users | To sum up. Client, partner and vendor each have distinct roles. R A C I makes clear who does the work, who owns it, who advises and who is told, with exactly one accountable person per activity. The client stays accountable for data quality, testing and business decisions. And release key users from daily work, because they make or break the project. Now try the exercises in this lesson, starting with matching each project role to its main responsibility. |
