# Video script: Functional vs Technical vs Techno-Functional Consultant

ERP Fundamentals lesson video (/videos/erpf-consultant-types.mp4), shown at the top of the lesson. Built from the lesson content; Odoo colours, nexusEPM logo, narration by Microsoft neural voice Neerja (Indian English) with captions.

| # | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title card: **Functional vs Technical vs Techno-Functional Consultant**. Three profiles, and why the bridge between them is in demand. | Welcome to lesson nine of E R P Fundamentals. In this video you will meet the three consultant profiles in an E R P project: functional, technical and techno-functional. You will see what each one does, and why the techno-functional consultant, who bridges both worlds, is so much in demand. |
| 2 | Step ?: **The missing backorder** (who, Odoo menu, impact) with an animated **Support ticket** card | A client of Northwind E R P Partners reports: when we deliver a partial order, the backorder does not appear on the warehouse screen. Who should take this ticket? A functional consultant might find it is a configuration choice. A technical consultant might find a bug in a customization. A techno-functional consultant can investigate both, and that is exactly why the role is in demand. |
| 3 | **Who does what**. Cards: Functional · Technical · Techno-functional | The functional consultant understands business processes like sales, purchasing, inventory and accounting. They run workshops, document requirements, design the future process, configure the system and train users, speaking the language of margins, lead times and credit limits. The technical consultant, the developer, builds customizations, integrations, reports and migration scripts, and handles performance, deployment and upgrades. The techno-functional consultant combines both, translating requirements into technical specifications and acting as the bridge between users and developers. |
| 4 | How deep is each profile? | Compare the skills. The functional consultant is deep in business processes, configuration and client communication, but has little or no programming. The technical consultant is deep in programming, data models and integrations. The techno-functional consultant is rarely the deepest in any single column, but is strong across almost all of them, and tests both at the functional and the unit level. That breadth is what lets them connect the two sides. |
| 5 | A day in the life | What does a techno-functional day look like? At nine, a workshop with the warehouse team on receiving and put away. At eleven, a technical specification for a custom delivery slip field. After lunch, preparing a product import file and fixing category mismatches. At three, a support ticket: check the configuration first, then inspect the record's technical fields. And at four thirty, reviewing a developer's change in the test environment against the acceptance criteria. |
| 6 | **Each answer narrows down where the fix belongs**. Animated flow: Reproduce in test? ▸ User error? ▸ Config or data? ▸ Escalate | Here is how tickets are diagnosed. First, can you reproduce the issue in the test environment? If not, gather the exact steps, the user and the record. If yes, is it user error or a training gap? Then train the user and update the manual. If not, is it a configuration or data problem? Fix it, testing first. Only if all of that is ruled out do you escalate to the developer or vendor. A techno-functional consultant can walk this whole tree alone. |
| 7 | Step ✓: **Solving the backorder ticket** (who, Odoo menu, impact) with an animated **Support ticket** card | Back to Northwind. The ticket went to a techno-functional consultant. She reproduced the issue in staging and checked the inventory settings; the backorder policy was correct. So she looked at customizations next, and found the warehouse screen was a customized list view whose filter excluded documents created from a backorder. She wrote a short defect note with the exact cause, the developer fixed it in an hour, and she retested and closed the ticket the same day. |
| 8 | **Escalate with the exact cause**. Cards: Closing as user error · A precise defect note | The common mistake is to close a ticket as user error, or to throw it over the wall to a developer with no analysis. Once configuration is ruled out, look at customizations, and escalate with the exact cause. A precise defect note lets the developer fix the problem in an hour instead of days. |
| 9 | **Career path**. Cards: Start functional · Start technical · Grow further | Many consultants start on the functional side because they have a business, accounting or supply chain background. Others start on the technical side, coming from software development. Both grow towards the techno-functional middle, and later paths include solution architect, project manager or practice lead. |
| 10 | **Key takeaways**. Cards: Functional vs technical · Techno-functional bridges both · Diagnose the layer · This academy's goal | To sum up. Functional means business process and configuration; technical means code, integration and data. The techno-functional consultant bridges both and turns requirements into specifications. Their key skill is diagnosing whether an issue is training, configuration, data or code. And building techno-functional consultants is exactly what this academy aims to do. Now try the exercises in this lesson, starting with matching each task to the right profile. |
