# Video script: Odoo crm, recorded live

Animated intro from the Odoo crm course, then a continuous live screen recording in an Odoo 20 database (fictional Northwind data), then a recap. Video /videos/odoo-crm-live.mp4 in the portal. Narration: Microsoft neural voice Neerja (Indian English).

| # | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title card: **Odoo CRM: Leads, Pipeline and Forecasting**. A short introduction, then the full process recorded live in Odoo 20. | Welcome to Odoo CRM. First a short introduction, and then we will take a real deal through the pipeline live in Odoo, from opportunity to won. |
| 2 | **The front door of Order to Cash**. Cards: Lead · Opportunity · Pipeline | Odoo CRM is the front door of the Order to Cash cycle. It records every potential deal from the first contact until it is won and handed to Sales as a quotation, or lost with a reason. Leads and opportunities are the same record with a different type, moving through the stages of the pipeline. |
| 3 | **What changes for a company like Northwind**. Cards: No lost enquiries · Pipeline visibility · A real forecast · Follow-up discipline | For a company like Northwind Office Supplies this means no more enquiries lost in a shared mailbox, one visible pipeline per team, a revenue forecast finance can plan with, and activities that make sure every customer gets a call back. |
| 4 | **From enquiry to won deal**. Animated flow: Enquiry ▸ Opportunity ▸ Qualified ▸ Quotation ▸ Sales Order ▸ Won | Here is the flow: an enquiry becomes an opportunity, it is qualified, a quotation is sent from Sales, and when the customer agrees the deal is won. Our scenario: Contoso asks for an annual toner contract worth about rupees one lakh fifty thousand. Let us do it live in Odoo. |
| 5 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Pipeline** | Let us do it live. In CRM, the pipeline shows every opportunity by stage. Click New to capture a fresh one. |
| 6 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Quick create** | Select the customer Contoso, name the opportunity, and enter the expected revenue of one lakh fifty thousand. Then add it. |
| 7 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Open it** | Open the new card. Here you see the revenue, the probability and the full history. |
| 8 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Qualify** | The need and the budget are confirmed, so move it to the Qualified stage. |
| 9 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Quotation** | Click New Quotation. Odoo opens a quotation with the customer and the salesperson already filled in. |
| 10 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Won** | Back on the opportunity, Contoso has signed. Click Won. The probability becomes one hundred percent. That is lead to win, live in Odoo. |
