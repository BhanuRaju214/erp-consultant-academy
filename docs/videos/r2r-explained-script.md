# Video script: Record to Report, explained

Animated explainer for the Record to Report business process (video /videos/r2r-explained.mp4 in the portal). Odoo colours, nexusEPM logo, narration by Microsoft neural voice Neerja (Indian English) with captions.

| # | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title card: **Record to Report**. How every transaction becomes reliable financial statements, and how Odoo closes the period. | Welcome. In this video you will learn the Record to Report process: how every business transaction is recorded, reconciled and summarised into financial statements, and how Odoo supports the period close. |
| 2 | **What is Record to Report?**. Cards: Record once · Reconcile · Report and close | Record to Report, or R2R, is the financial accounting cycle. Every business transaction is recorded once, in the right account and period, reconciled to external evidence such as the bank, customers, vendors and tax, and summarised into financial statements. Management, auditors, tax authorities and investors must be able to rely on them. |
| 3 | **Who takes part?**. Cards: CFO / Finance Manager · GL Accountant · AR Accountant · AP Accountant · Treasury Officer · Tax Accountant · External Auditor | Several people take part. The finance manager owns the close calendar and approves the statements. The general ledger accountant maintains the chart of accounts and posts accruals. The receivable and payable accountants manage customers and vendors. Treasury reconciles the bank, the tax accountant files the return, and the external auditor verifies the result. |
| 4 | **Nine steps from record to report**. Animated flow: Chart of accounts ▸ Opening balances ▸ Invoices & bills ▸ Journal entries ▸ Receivables ▸ Payables ▸ Bank reconcile ▸ Statements ▸ Close period | Here is the big picture: nine steps. Design the chart of accounts, load opening balances, record customer and vendor transactions, post manual journal entries, manage receivables and payables, reconcile the bank, prepare the financial statements, and close the period. In Odoo most entries come from the operational apps, so finance mainly reviews, reconciles and closes. |
| 5 | Step 1–2: **Chart of accounts and opening balances** (who, Odoo menu, impact) with an animated **Opening balances** card | Steps one and two. The chart of accounts lists every ledger account and must meet local statutory needs while staying simple. Clearview Advisory installs the fiscal localisation and handles its departments as analytic accounts. At go-live, forty seven open invoices and nineteen open bills are imported individually, the remaining balances are posted, and the migration clearing account ends at zero. |
| 6 | Step 3: **Record customer and vendor transactions** (who, Odoo menu, impact) with an animated **January activity** card | Step three. In a modern E R P most entries originate in the operational apps: invoices from Sales, bills from Purchase, stock valuation from Inventory. Accountants check that each document carries the right accounts, taxes and analytic tags. In January, Clearview issues twenty eight invoices and receives forty one vendor bills. Good sub-ledger discipline means fewer manual journals. |
| 7 | Step 4: **Post manual journal entries** (who, Odoo menu, impact) with an animated **Journal Entry · MISC** card | Step four. Some transactions have no operational document: accrued expenses, prepaid expenses, depreciation and corrections. They are posted as manual journal entries with clear references and support. Clearview's office rent bill arrives late, so the accountant accrues it, and the entry reverses automatically on the first of February. |
| 8 | Step 5–6: **Manage receivables and payables** (who, Odoo menu, impact) with an animated **Aged reports** card | Steps five and six. Receivables are reviewed for overdue and disputed invoices, and customers are reminded. Clearview's aged receivable is two lakh eighty six thousand, of which thirty one thousand is over sixty days, and a four thousand provision is booked for a disputed invoice. On the payables side, every valid liability is recorded, and the aged payable equals the payables account. |
| 9 | Step 7: **Reconcile bank accounts** (who, Odoo menu, impact) with an animated **Bank · January** card | Step seven. Every bank statement line is matched to a payment, invoice, bill or expense. Clearview syncs two hundred and fourteen bank lines; reconciliation models clear one hundred and eighty automatically, and thirty four are matched by hand. At period end the book balance equals the statement: rupees one lakh eighty eight thousand four hundred. |
| 10 | Step 8: **Prepare financial statements** (who, Odoo menu, impact) with an animated **Profit and Loss · January** card | Step eight. Once the ledgers are reconciled, the trial balance is reviewed and the statements are produced: balance sheet, profit and loss and cash flow. Clearview's revenue is three lakh thirty eight thousand and net profit thirty eight thousand seven hundred and fifty, eleven and a half percent. Variances against budget are analysed and explained. |
| 11 | Step 9: **Close the period** (who, Odoo menu, impact) with an animated **January close** card | Step nine. After review the period is locked, so no one can post or change entries in it. The tax return is filed: forty eight thousand of output tax minus fourteen thousand two hundred and fifty of input tax leaves thirty three thousand seven hundred and fifty to pay. The CFO sets the lock date to the thirty first of January. Days to close: four. |
| 12 | **Typical closing entries**: 1. Accrued rent at month end · 2. Depreciation · 3. Tax closing | Let's look at typical closing entries. Rent not yet billed is accrued: debit rent expense three thousand, credit accrued liabilities, reversed on the first of next month. Depreciation of eight hundred comes from the asset board. And the tax closing clears output and input tax into the net amount payable to the authority. Remember: at period end, every control account must match its supporting report. |
| 13 | **Common risks, and how Odoo controls them**. Cards: Unreconciled opening balances · Postings into closed periods · Sub-ledger ≠ control account · Bank lines piling up | What can go wrong? Opening balances that do not reconcile: load them through a clearing account that ends at zero. Postings into closed periods: set lock dates immediately after the close. Sub-ledgers that differ from control accounts: prevent manual journals on receivable and payable accounts. And unreconciled bank lines: import daily and use reconciliation models. |
| 14 | **Record, reconcile, report, close**. Animated flow: Accounts ▸ Opening ▸ Transactions ▸ Journals ▸ AR ▸ AP ▸ Bank ▸ Statements ▸ Close | To recap: chart of accounts, opening balances, transactions, journals, receivables, payables, bank, statements and close.
