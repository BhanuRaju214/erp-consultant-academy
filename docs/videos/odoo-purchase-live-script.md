# Video script: Odoo purchase, recorded live

Animated intro from the Odoo purchase course, then a continuous live screen recording in an Odoo 20 database (fictional Northwind data), then a recap. Video /videos/odoo-purchase-live.mp4 in the portal. Narration: Microsoft neural voice Neerja (Indian English).

| # | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title card: **Odoo Purchase: RFQ to Vendor Bill**. A short introduction, then the full process recorded live in Odoo 20. | Welcome to Odoo Purchase. First a short introduction, and then we will run the whole process live in Odoo, from the request for quotation to the posted vendor bill. |
| 2 | **Buying from your vendors**. Cards: RFQ · Purchase order · Vendor bill | The Odoo Purchase app manages buying from vendors: requests for quotation, confirmed purchase orders, the link to receipts in Inventory, and the control of vendor bills in Accounting. An R F Q and a purchase order are the same record in different states, so the reference stays the same when you confirm it. |
| 3 | **What changes for a company like Northwind**. Cards: Negotiated prices · Control large orders · No stock-outs · Pay only what arrived | For a company like Northwind Office Supplies this means negotiated prices proposed on every R F Q, manager approval for orders above five lakh rupees, reordering rules that buy paper before it runs out, and vendor bills paid only for what was actually received. |
| 4 | **From RFQ to payment**. Animated flow: RFQ ▸ RFQ Sent ▸ Purchase Order ▸ Receipt ▸ Vendor Bill ▸ Payment | Here is the document flow: R F Q, R F Q sent, purchase order, receipt, vendor bill and payment. Our scenario: Northwind orders five hundred reams of A4 paper from Paper Mills Inc. Let us do it live in Odoo. |
| 5 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Create an RFQ** | Let us do it live. In the Purchase app, open Requests for Quotation and click New. |
| 6 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Vendor** | Type the vendor name and select Paper Mills Inc. Payment terms and currency come from the vendor. |
| 7 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Product** | Add a product line and select A4 copy paper. The unit price comes from the vendor price list on the product. |
| 8 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Quantity** | Northwind needs five hundred reams. Type the quantity and the totals update. |
| 9 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Deliver to** | Choose the Northwind Main receipts, so the paper arrives in the right warehouse. |
| 10 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Confirm the order** | Paper Mills has agreed. Click Confirm Order. The request becomes a purchase order, and Odoo creates the receipt. |
| 11 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Receive** | When the truck arrives, click Receive and validate the receipt. Five hundred reams enter stock. |
| 12 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Vendor bill** | Now switch to Accounting and create a new vendor bill for Paper Mills. |
| 13 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Link the order** | In Auto-Complete, pick the purchase order. Odoo copies the received quantities into the bill. |
| 14 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Post the bill** | Enter the vendor invoice number and the bill date, then confirm. The bill is posted and ready to pay. That is procure to pay, live in Odoo. |
| 15 | **What you just did in Odoo**. Animated flow: RFQ ▸ Confirm order ▸ Receive ▸ Vendor bill ▸ Post bill | To recap: you created the R F Q, confirmed the order, received the paper, created the vendor bill in Accounting with Auto-Complete, and posted it.
