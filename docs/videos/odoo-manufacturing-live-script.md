# Video script: Odoo manufacturing, recorded live

Animated intro from the Odoo manufacturing course, then a continuous live screen recording in an Odoo 20 database (fictional Northwind data), then a recap. Video /videos/odoo-manufacturing-live.mp4 in the portal. Narration: Microsoft neural voice Neerja (Indian English).

| # | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title card: **Odoo Manufacturing: BoMs, Work Centers and Orders**. A short introduction, then the full process recorded live in Odoo 20. | Welcome to Odoo Manufacturing. First a short introduction, and then we will run a manufacturing order live in Odoo, from draft to done. |
| 2 | **What it is made of, how, when, and at what cost**. Cards: Bill of Materials · Work center · Manufacturing order | The Odoo Manufacturing app records what a product is made of, where and how long it takes to make, when it must be made, and what it cost. It sits directly on top of Inventory: a manufacturing order consumes components and brings finished goods into stock. |
| 3 | **What changes for a furniture maker**. Cards: Real product cost · No line stops · No lost job cards · Orders trigger production | For a furniture maker this means knowing what a kitchen cabinet really costs, no production line stopping because panels ran out, work orders instead of lost paper job cards, and customer orders that create manufacturing orders automatically. |
| 4 | **From demand to finished goods**. Animated flow: Demand ▸ Manufacturing Order ▸ Confirmed ▸ In Progress ▸ Done ▸ Deliver | Here is the flow: a manufacturing order is created and confirmed, which reserves the components. Production is recorded, and when the order is done the components leave stock and the finished goods come in. Our scenario: Northwind builds five kitchen cabinets in the Northwind Main warehouse. Let us do it live in Odoo. |
| 5 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **New MO** | Let us do it live. In Manufacturing, open Manufacturing Orders and click New. |
| 6 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Product** | Select the kitchen cabinet. Odoo loads its bill of materials with all the components. |
| 7 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Quantity** | Set the quantity to five. The component quantities scale with it. |
| 8 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Operation type** | In the Miscellaneous tab, choose the Northwind Main manufacturing operation, so components come from that warehouse. |
| 9 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Confirm** | Click Confirm. Odoo reserves the components in stock. |
| 10 | Live Odoo 20 screen recording: **Produce** | The cabinets are built. Click Produce. Components are consumed and five cabinets enter stock at their real cost. That is plan to produce, live in Odoo. |
| 11 | **What you just did in Odoo**. Animated flow: Manufacturing order ▸ Quantity ▸ Operation type ▸ Confirm ▸ Produce ▸ Done | To recap: you created the manufacturing order, set the quantity to five cabinets, chose the operation type for Northwind Main, confirmed it, produced, and marked it done. Now practise it yourself with the click-through simulators in the Odoo Manufacturing course. |
